## Grading the call

**Call: not sized. Outcome: MISS.**

The right-tail risk materialized exactly as flagged: clean beat on every line, the Meta partnership overrode the capex concern, and the stock ripped through the priced-in move range. Every bear point (revenue miss history, capex overhang, sector peer CRWV selloff) was negated by the quality of the print.

What the agent got right:
- Correctly identified asymmetric skew as the key risk
- Correctly noted capex raise as probable (it went to $25B)
- Correctly flagged CRWV selloff-on-beat as a sector precedent

What the agent missed:
- Underweighted the probability of a meta-level partnership announcement that rerates the customer concentration question entirely
- The "three misses in four quarters" history didn't account for the velocity of the ARR inflection -- $1.92B ARR run-rate in Q1 is a different company than the one that missed Q4 2024 revenue
- CRWV sold off on capex; NBIS got credit for capex because Meta partnership absorbed the funding overhang narrative

**Portable lesson:** When the pre-print run is +50% AND fundamental inflection (ARR acceleration, margin swing, major customer announcement) is all converging simultaneously, the downside-asymmetry read may be wrong. Momentum that large can be early signal, not froth. The agent should have sized a small tranche (half-pilot) rather than zero.
