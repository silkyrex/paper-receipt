## Sector setup

Space stocks are running on two converging catalysts:

- **SpaceX IPO speculation.** A widely-reported potential $75B SpaceX listing is pulling capital into every adjacent space name -- pure momentum trade where investors front-run the IPO basket effect.
- **ARKX +74% YoY.** Top holdings include LHX, KTOS, RKLB, TER, DE. The ETF is a flow proxy showing the sector's institutional bid is real, not just retail.
- **Defense space spend.** Hypersonic test contracts, NASA ETF launch, and broad defense modernization are flowing into commercial space caps.

Neither PL nor RKLB is currently in the locker. This brief is locker_agent surfacing both as candidates worth a pilot position before formally adding them to the watch list.
